Frequently asked questions

Great questions from our clients, compiled to help you understand what the fund is about.

Does 10 years maturity mean the fund will close after 10 years and investors need to re-apply?

Yes. The fund closes and is liquidated, and clients can take their investment back or switch into another fund. This is a requirement of the licensing body that issues the regulatory license for the fund.

Is there a limit on how much each client can invest?

The minimum entry for each investor is 100,000 GBP. Internally we monitor that no single investor holds more than 5% of the fund. This is a guide, and our fund managers use their analysis to keep the fund from being dominated by a few investors. The maximum per investor is 10 million pounds.

Why is the fund specific to Bordeaux portfolios?

Bordeaux wines are generally quicker to sell back to the market, which lets the fund buy and sell quickly to maximise client returns. Other regions can take longer to sell because demand is weaker. Bordeaux also has recognised classifications and laws on how wine is made, which makes it more exclusive and in demand.

Why are Bordeaux wines valued in GBP?

Wines stored in the UK are valued in GBP and wines stored in France in Euros. The GBP fund is bigger because we buy more wine in GBP. Investors can invest money or wine into either fund. Clients' wines in Bordeaux are converted into the Euro fund, and wines in the UK are added to the GBP fund.

What does “wine fund liquidity” mean?

It is the cash held in the fund's bank account in GBP or Euros. The USD account is kept separate for fees. We use liquidity to buy new wines; with none, we wait until wines are sold. The fund manager keeps enough cash for investors who wish to liquidate in the short term.

What is the rationale behind the minimum investment per investor?

The minimum is set by the Labuan Financial Services Authority (LFSA), which issued our license. An investor can build a portfolio through individual cases of wine, then switch it into the fund once the minimum is met.

When dividends are reinvested, are there charges?

There is no charge for reinvesting dividends. There is a 10% fee on the fund's profit, deducted from the total value of the client's units.

Does my investment stay in its own currency?

Yes. The GBP or Euro fund always stays in the investor's respective currency.

How does the 4-week liquidation work?

When an investor sells less than 10,000 of their units, it takes 4 weeks to receive the cash. This gives the fund time to sell wines if liquidity is low, and avoids selling below market price to raise cash urgently.

Does storage in bond exempt wine trading from customs duties and taxes?

Yes, exempt in the UK and France.

Are the annual, income, wedding and pension plans available, and are the charges the same?

Yes. These are good ways to plan investments into the fund at different periods to fit your lifestyle and goals.

Can I transfer my current portfolio instead of cash, and how is it valued?

Yes. The wine is valued at the time using the bid price on our website, investintowine.com. The ask price is an indicative price to repurchase a wine; the bid price is the price to sell it. The 3% entry fee applies, so a portfolio worth 100,000 GBP would receive 97,000 units (1 GBP = 1 unit).

How is the 10% performance fee calculated?

The financial year runs January to December, and a performance report is produced every January. The fee is deducted from the gross yearly return and taken from the fund's gains only. If the fund makes no return, no performance fee is payable.

Are storage fees deducted before the performance fee, and how often are they charged?

Yes. Storage fees are charged every six months, on the same billing period as a Sure Holdings individual portfolio, because Sure Holdings administers the storage facilities. The management fee is also deducted every six months.

Are withdrawals done by selling units?

Yes. You sell units to liquidate your position. It takes 4 weeks if the amount is under 10,000 GBP or Euro.

Can I add to my investment later, and is the entry fee charged again?

Yes. Additions do not need to be in increments of 100,000 GBP; any amount you are comfortable with works. The entry fee is paid whenever an investment is made.

How is the price of a unit calculated?

If the fund increases 2%, the unit value goes from 1.00 to 1.02 pounds, and new units are bought at 1.02. The fund is valued every month, and the latest valuation is used for any new investor so they pay the correct price per unit.
